# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want NoxaPlus Ledger to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience level, language, nationality, gender identity, disability or background. Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation harassment-free and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback about code and ideas, not about people.
- Respect different levels of experience and different use cases for a personal finance tool.
- Keep discussions on topic and avoid sharing anyone's private financial data.
- Accept maintainers' decisions gracefully, and ask for clarification when you disagree.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, including ledger screenshots or backups, without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any project space, as well as in public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns to **NoxaPlus2025@gmail.com**. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or block contributors whose behavior violates this code.
